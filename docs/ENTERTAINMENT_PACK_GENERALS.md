# 娱乐包将领说明 / Entertainment Pack Generals

## 中文说明

娱乐包 V0.1 从纯净 HotA 1.8.0 独立构建，不包含正式补丁 V1.52 的原创英雄内容，两条补丁线不可互相覆盖安装。

### 丢丢特

- 初始技能：初级箭术、初级水系魔法；魔法书自带魔法神箭。
- 初始部队：三组未升级精怪，每组 30–40 只。
- 特长效果：精怪和大精怪速度 +1；英雄每升 1 级，提高其 10% 的攻击和防御（向上取整）。

### 斯强克

- 保留原版头像、传记、职业、初始技能、初始部队和僵尸特长图标。
- 特长效果：麾下的行尸和僵尸每次攻击后，都会对目标释放一次雷鸣爆弹（内爆），造成“英雄当前力量 × 75 + 100”点伤害，不消耗魔法值。
- 主动攻击和反击均可触发；法术有效性、动画与结算采用游戏原生内爆规则。
- 普通雷鸟仍保持原版闪电、20% 触发概率和每只 10 点伤害。

## English Description

Entertainment Pack V0.1 is an independent branch built from clean HotA 1.8.0. It does not contain the original heroes from the formal V1.52 patch, and the two patch lines must not be installed over one another.

### Diu Diu

- Starts with Basic Archery and Basic Water Magic; the spell book contains Magic Arrow.
- Starts with three stacks of 30–40 unupgraded Gremlins.
- Specialty: Gremlins and Master Gremlins gain +1 Speed and receive a rounded-up 10% Attack and Defense bonus for every hero level.

### Straker

- Retains his original portrait, biography, class, starting skills, starting army, and Zombie specialty icon.
- Specialty: after every attack, his Walking Dead and Zombies cast Implosion on the target for `75 × current Spell Power + 100` damage without spending spell points.
- Active attacks and retaliations can trigger the effect; validity, animation, and settlement use the game's native Implosion rules.
- Native Thunderbirds retain Lightning Bolt, their original 20% chance, and 10 damage per living Thunderbird.
