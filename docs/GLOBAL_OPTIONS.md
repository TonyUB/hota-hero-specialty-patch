# 全局按钮说明 / Global Options

[返回首页 / Back to the project page](../README.md) · [English](#global-options)

在 **HotA 1.8.0 中文 HD 版**进入 **新游戏 → 单人场景 → 其它选项…**，可分别勾选四个按钮。点「确定」后生效，点「取消」会放弃本次改动。首次安装时四项均默认关闭，原有的对战规则仍可单独选择。

这些选项是本机全局设置。确认后会记住选择，载入存档也会使用本机现有设置。选项不会写入单个存档或在联机玩家之间同步，本版验证范围为 HD 单人场景。

## 1. 取消养步规则

开启后，人类玩家所有英雄在过夜结算陆地行动力时，兵种速度基数固定按 **11 速（2000 点）**计算。即使队里有慢速兵，也不用为了第二天的基础行动力而临时移走它。后勤术、宝物与英雄特长的行动力加成仍会计入，海上行动力、电脑英雄以及当天已经结算的行动力照旧。

## 2. 经验翻倍

开启后，人类与电脑英雄新获得的经验都会翻倍。战斗、事件、学习石、宝箱等通过正常结算发放的经验均适用。先算学习能力等原有加成，再乘以 2。例如，一次奖励结算后原应得到 1600 点经验，开启后实际得到 3200 点。

已有经验和地图预设的初始经验不会追溯翻倍，原等级上限继续生效。部分奖励窗口仍显示原始数值，可以打开英雄面板核对实际入账经验。

## 3. 组合宝物只占一格

完整组合宝装备后只占一格。你可以把它放在任一普通身体装备格或五个杂物格之一，共 **14 个普通装备格**。魔法书和战争机器使用各自的专用格。组合宝保有原来的属性和特殊效果，拆出的普通组件仍按各自的部位装备。

要拆分组合宝，先把它移入背包，再右键确认。若它还装备在身上，游戏会提示「请先将组合宝物放入背包，再进行拆分。」拆分时所有组件进入背包，组合宝腾出的那一格也计入容量。例如，拆出六件组件只需要再有五个空格。容量不足时显示原提示「你的行囊已满。」，不消耗或拆散宝物。英雄界面和英雄交换界面都适用。

## 4. 卷轴学习

有魔法书的英雄可以在英雄界面或英雄交换界面右键装备栏、背包中的魔法卷轴。游戏会显示卷轴的魔法，并询问「是否消耗这张魔法卷轴，永久学会其中的魔法？」确认后消耗选中的一张卷轴，魔法永久加入该英雄的魔法书，不受智慧术或学习等级限制。

取消、没有魔法书或已经永久学会该魔法时，卷轴会保留。关闭选项后，右键恢复原本的查看操作，已经学会的魔法仍保存在存档中。凛冬裁决仍由寒冰之剑授予。

## Global Options

Open **New Game → Single Scenario → Other Options** in Chinese HotA 1.8.0 HD. Each of the four options can be enabled independently. Confirm to apply your changes, or cancel to discard them. All four are off on a fresh installation. The tournament rules toggle remains available.

The options apply to the local installation, including games loaded from saves. The settings are remembered locally and do not travel with a save or synchronize across multiplayer clients. This release was tested in HD single-player scenarios.

### 1. Ignore slowest troop for land movement

At the overnight land movement refresh, all human-controlled heroes use creature Speed 11 as their baseline, worth 2,000 movement points. Logistics, artifacts, and hero specialty bonuses still apply. Sea movement, AI heroes, and movement points already calculated for the day follow their existing rules.

### 2. Double Experience

New experience awarded to human and AI heroes is doubled after existing bonuses, including Learning. This applies to regular awards such as battles, events, Learning Stones, and treasure chests. An award that would give 1,600 XP after bonuses gives 3,200 XP with the option enabled. Earlier experience, map-defined starting XP, and the original level limit are unaffected. Some reward dialogs still show the base award. Check the hero screen for the actual total.

### 3. One Slot per Combination Artifact

An assembled combination artifact uses one of the 14 regular equipment slots, including all five miscellaneous slots. Spellbook and war machine slots remain reserved. The artifact retains its normal bonuses and special effects. Individual components still use their original equipment slots.

Move the artifact to the backpack before right-clicking to disassemble it. All components go into the backpack, and the slot freed by the combination artifact counts toward the required space. For example, six components require five additional empty slots. If there is insufficient space, the game shows its usual full-backpack message and keeps the combination intact. This also works in the hero exchange screen.

### 4. Learn from Scrolls

A hero with a spellbook can right-click an equipped scroll or a scroll in the backpack, including in the hero exchange screen. The prompt shows the spell and asks for confirmation. Confirming consumes the selected scroll and teaches its spell permanently to the hero who holds it, regardless of Wisdom or spell level.

Canceling the prompt, lacking a spellbook, or already knowing the spell permanently leaves the scroll intact. Disabling the option restores the standard right-click inspection. Previously learned spells remain in the save. Winter Judgment is still granted by the Sword of Frost.
