中文
1. 埃尔芙（Elf Queen）— 仙灵与妖精特长：麾下仙灵和妖精的最小、最大杀伤力均 +1，速度 +1；初始兵力固定为 25 仙灵 / 25 仙灵 / 25 仙灵，且不修改全局兵种数据。
2. 尤兰德（Uland，壁垒）与阿斯特拉（Astra，港口）— 治愈特长：保留原版治愈、解除负面状态及英雄原有治疗特长增幅；目标兵队至少有 1 只存活且本场已有阵亡时，先治疗现存单位，再将剩余治疗量按原生转世重生资格永久复活同一兵队（亡灵等禁止目标只治疗、不复活）；高级水系魔法的群体治愈逐队结算。阿斯特拉的初始技能改为初级智慧术 + 初级水系魔法。
3. 阿德拉（Adela）— 祝福特长：保留 HotA 1.8.0 原生祝福特长及等级成长效果；仅阿德拉施放祝福时最终魔法值消耗固定为 0，其他法术与其他英雄的魔法消耗不变。

English
1. Elf Queen — Pixie and Sprite specialty: Pixies and Sprites in her army gain +1 minimum and maximum damage and +1 Speed; her starting army is fixed at 25 Pixies / 25 Pixies / 25 Pixies, without changing the global creature data.
2. Uland (Rampart) and Astra (Cove) — Cure specialty: Native Cure healing, negative-effect removal, and each hero's original Cure-specialty scaling are preserved. If the target stack has at least one living creature and has suffered casualties in the current battle, living creatures are healed first and the remaining healing permanently resurrects casualties in that same stack through the native Resurrection eligibility rules; forbidden targets such as undead are healed but not resurrected. Expert Water Magic mass Cure resolves each eligible living stack separately. Astra starts with Basic Wisdom and Basic Water Magic.
3. Adela — Bless specialty: Her native HotA 1.8.0 Bless specialty and level scaling are preserved; only Bless cast by Adela has a final mana cost of 0, while her other spells and Bless cast by other heroes keep their normal costs.
