# HOTA_NEW_HERO_V1.62

2026-11-17 · HotA 1.8.0 中文 HD 版

- 新增棱堡英雄姬嘉，初始携带寒冰之剑。持剑者可施放凛冬裁决，姬嘉还会随所学技能获得额外加成。
- 「其它选项」加入四个独立开关。取消养步规则、经验翻倍、组合宝物只占一格和卷轴学习的用法见[全局按钮说明](https://github.com/TonyUB/hota-hero-specialty-patch/blob/main/docs/GLOBAL_OPTIONS.md)。
- 更新中英文门帘及英雄手册，姬嘉列在首位。

## English

- Adds Kija of Bulwark. She starts with the Sword of Frost, which grants Winter Judgment to its wielder. Kija unlocks extra bonuses through her skills.
- Adds four switches under Other Options. See the [Global Options Guide](https://github.com/TonyUB/hota-hero-specialty-patch/blob/main/docs/GLOBAL_OPTIONS.md#global-options) for activation, effects and limits.
- Updates the bilingual project page and handbook with Kija first.
