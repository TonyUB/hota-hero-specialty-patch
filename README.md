## 埃尔芙（精灵女王）

埃尔芙是妖精一族的女王，她的名字长久以来只存在于吟游诗人的古老歌谣中。她以绝世容貌与卓越的战术才能闻名。当埃拉西亚陷入危难时，她打破避世誓言，响应元素城的召唤，亲率部众为这片大陆而战。

**特长效果：** 仙灵和妖精杀伤力 +1，速度 +1。

**初始兵力：** 25 仙灵 / 25 仙灵 / 25 仙灵。

## 尤兰德 / 阿斯特拉

**尤兰德：** 尤兰德在选择德鲁伊之路前，曾在战场上做过大量的治疗工作，他在军队里学到的经验使他成为了今天出色的领导者。

**阿斯特拉：** 阿斯特拉从不急着向别人说起她的往事，以致于很少有人知道她的来历。但谁都知道她为了学到神秘的水系魔法几乎访问了这片大陆所有的地方。后来她成为了海洋女祭司，在这里实现了精通水系魔法的夙愿。

**特长效果：** 治愈魔法可以永久复活友方单位。

## 阿德拉

阿德拉最初只是在战斗前贡献自己的技能。如果她无法说服指挥官避免战斗，她至少会在部队进入战斗状态时为他们准备祝福。她被任命为白石守备部队的指挥官。

**特长效果：** 施放祝福时不消耗魔法值。施放祝福时，英雄等级每增加 n 级，效果提高 10%，其中 n 是目标生物的等级。

## Elf Queen

The Elf Queen is the queen of the fairies. For ages, her name existed only in the ancient songs of wandering bards. Renowned for her peerless beauty and outstanding tactical ability, she broke her vow of seclusion when Erathia fell into peril, answered the call of the Conflux, and personally led her people into battle for the land.

**Specialty effect:** Pixies and Sprites gain +1 Damage and +1 Speed.

**Starting army:** 25 Pixies / 25 Pixies / 25 Pixies.

## Uland / Astra

**Uland:** Before choosing the path of the Druid, Uland spent a great deal of time healing the wounded on the battlefield. The experience he gained in the army made him the outstanding leader he is today.

**Astra:** Astra never hurries to tell others about her past, so few people know where she came from. Everyone knows, however, that she traveled to nearly every corner of the continent in search of the secrets of Water Magic. She later became an Ocean Priestess and fulfilled her ambition of mastering Water Magic.

**Specialty effect:** Cure can permanently resurrect friendly units.

## Adela

Adela originally contributed her skills only before battle. If she could not persuade a commander to avoid combat, she would at least prepare the troops with Bless before they entered battle. She was appointed commander of the guard at Castle White.

**Specialty effect:** Bless costs no spell points when cast. Its effect increases by 10% for every n hero levels, where n is the target creature's level.
